## Questline

**Status:** In development

Build-your-own-adventure with real dice underneath: d20 mechanics and an AI Dungeon Master spin branching quests you actually play, not just read — all running as a single file in your browser.

### What's new
- Five hand-crafted offline campaigns with looping music beds
- Live Adventure mode: rotating AI models as DM, deterministic engine owns the state
- *The Book of Truth* redesign in progress — form-fitted adaptations of the author's prose
